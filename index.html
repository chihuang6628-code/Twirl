親愛的 Chevening 校友們：

還記得當年踏上英國土地、開啟人生新篇章的那一刻嗎？

那些在英國獲得的知識、結交的朋友、建立的連結，以及意想不到的機會，至今仍持續影響著我們。如今，我們也有機會把這份珍貴的禮物傳下去，讓下一位台灣學者，擁有改變人生的可能。

如果我們曾經因為 Chevening 而有了更多可能，現在，就讓我們一起為下一代創造這樣的機會。

1一份已經啟程的禮物

台灣每年最多有 5 個 Chevening 獎學金名額，由英國政府透過英國在台辦事處支持，並獲得台達電子文教基金會、王文洋博士及 TutorABC 的慷慨贊助。

5→6
2026 年，透過與台北市英僑商務協會（BCCT）的合作，台灣新增了第 6 個 Chevening 獎學金名額。
這份努力已經有了成果——2026 年獲得新增名額的學者，已經啟程前往英國攻讀碩士。

2每一份心意，都能匯聚成改變

台灣有超過 250 位 Chevening 校友。有人剛從英國返台，有人已在各自的領域耕耘多年；我們走過不同的道路，卻共享著一段珍貴的經歷。

這次，BCCT 誠摯邀請各位 CAT 校友，以自由認捐的方式，一起支持 2026 年新增的獎學金名額，以及這位今年已經啟程前往英國的學者。

捐款單位
NT$4,500 （約 £100）
認捐份數
不限，完全自願

  • 捐款以 NT$4,500 為一單位，歡迎認捐多個單位（如 NT$9,000、NT$13,500），完全自願。
  • 募款目標約 £25,000，期盼匯聚校友的力量，共同支持這個新增的獎學金名額。

不論認捐一份或多份，都能讓這份集體力量更往前一步。只要願意，每一份支持都值得感謝。

正如發起這次 Joint Scholarship 的 BCCT 主席暨 TutorABC 執行長 Samuel Yang 所說：

「只要每個人每年捐出一點點，哪怕只是 100 英鎊，我們就能匯聚可觀的力量，改變許多人的人生。」

— Samuel Yang，BCCT 主席暨 TutorABC 執行長

Dear Chevening Alumni,

Do you remember the moment you first set foot in the UK and began a new chapter of your life?

The knowledge we gained, the friendships we made, the connections we built and the unexpected opportunities we encountered in the UK continue to shape who we are today. Now, we have the opportunity to pass this precious gift on and help another Taiwanese scholar discover what is possible.

If Chevening opened up new possibilities for us, let’s now work together to create the same opportunity for the next generation.

1A Gift That Has Already Set a Journey in Motion

Taiwan receives up to five Chevening Scholarships each year, supported by the UK Government through the British Office Taipei, with generous contributions from the Delta Electronics Foundation, Dr Winston Wong and TutorABC.

5→6
In 2026, through a partnership with the British Chamber of Commerce in Taipei (BCCT), Taiwan received a sixth Chevening Scholarship.
This effort has already made a difference: the scholar awarded the additional scholarship in 2026 has departed for the UK to pursue a master’s degree.

2Every Contribution Adds Up to Change

Taiwan is home to more than 250 Chevening alumni. Some have only recently returned from the UK, while others have been contributing to their fields for many years. We may have taken different paths, but we share a precious experience.

BCCT warmly invites CAT alumni to make a voluntary contribution to support the additional scholarship established in 2026 and the scholar who has already begun their journey to the UK this year.

Giving unit
NT$4,500 (approx. £100)
Number of units
Any, entirely voluntary
Fundraising target
Approx. £25,000
  • Contributions are made in units of NT$4,500, and you are welcome to give more than one unit (e.g. NT$9,000 or NT$13,500). Giving is entirely voluntary.
  • Fundraising target: approximately £25,000, with the hope of bringing alumni together to support the additional scholarship established in 2026.

Whether you give one unit or several, your contribution helps move this collective effort forward. Every contribution is sincerely appreciated.

As Samuel Yang, Chairman of BCCT and CEO of TutorABC, who initiated this Joint Scholarship, puts it:

“If everyone gives a little each year, even just £100, together we can build considerable support and change many people’s lives.”

— Samuel Yang, Chairman of BCCT and CEO of TutorABC
